Click a step to switch it on; shift-click, right-click or long-press to make it loud or soft. space plays and stops, 1 to 8 hit the voices (with Rec on they are written into the beat), [ and ] change beat, T taps the tempo, ctrl+Z undoes. Click a voice name to select it for the two mint knobs. Kit swaps the drum set, Note gives steps a pitch, and ? on the panel explains the rest. Everything is saved on this device; nothing is sent anywhere.
